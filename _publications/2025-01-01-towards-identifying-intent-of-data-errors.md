This paper explores the problem of identifying the intent behind data errors — whether errors are accidental, systematic, or malicious. Understanding the provenance and intent of data errors is a critical step towards more effective data cleaning and data quality management.

**Authors:** Mohamed Abdelmaksoud, Konrad Rieck, Ziawasch Abedjan

**Venue:** Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment (PVLDB), ISSN 2150-8097
